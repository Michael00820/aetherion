Open THIS android folder in Android Studio (File → Open → android).

Do not open the repo root as the Gradle project.

Then follow ANDROID.md (build) and play/PLAY_CONSOLE.md (publish).

Package: com.aetherion.observatory
First signed file Play wants: android/app/build/outputs/bundle/release/app-release.aab
You must create the upload keystore on your machine (scripts/create-upload-keystore.sh).
Never put the .jks in email, git, or Drive public folders.
