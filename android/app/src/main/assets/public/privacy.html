Aetherion Privacy Policy

Last updated: 4 September 2026

Aetherion is an observatory of Ethiopian time, planetary hours, the solar system, and Tewahedo scripture. It is built to work on your device.

What we collect

The app does not require an account. Preferences (theme, simulation speed, last place) stay on your device.

Location

If you tap “Your horizon”, Aetherion reads your approximate coordinates once so it can compute local sunrise and sunset for planetary hours. Location is not uploaded, stored on a server, or sold. You may refuse the prompt and keep a pinned city.

Network

Scripture, calendar, and orbits work offline after install. Optional chapter translation uses a network request only when you ask for it.

Children

Aetherion is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on github.com/Michael00820/aetherion.
